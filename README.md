## Featured project

### [Cricket Ad Muter](https://github.com/absinha86/cricket-ad-muter-extension)

[![Cricket Ad Muter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/absinha86/cricket-ad-muter-extension/main/store/social-preview.png)](https://github.com/absinha86/cricket-ad-muter-extension)

A Chrome extension that mutes ad breaks during live cricket on JioHotstar and SonyLIV, and brings the sound back the moment play resumes.

- Detects each ad as it starts: from the site's own tracking request on JioHotstar, and from the video player's ad marker on SonyLIV.
- A badge on the video with the ad countdown, even in fullscreen, plus one-key "unmute this break" or "mute now".
- Private by design: no tracking, no accounts, nothing leaves the browser. Detection fixes ship as a data-only rules file, without a store update.

[Source](https://github.com/absinha86/cricket-ad-muter-extension) · [Download v1.1.0](https://github.com/absinha86/cricket-ad-muter-extension/releases/latest) · Chrome Web Store: coming soon
